About me

Hello, my name is Amandine and I'm a student at Creajeux(Nîmes/France) for my 4th and last year. I'm 21 years old and since childhood, my dream has always been to participate in the creation of a video game. I love to code, I'm always doing my best and I'm curious to learn new things. For me, video games was a way to create a universe, with life, and make the player able to be part of this univers by embodying the main character. This aspect of video games was really what I was looking for. But learning more about coding, I started loving coding tools and things that's help people making games. I'm also a big fan of knitting and sewing, to take a break from screens and express my creativity. I love board games too.
